HOW TO PLAY

Move: joystick or A/D. Jump: JUMP or Space. Attack/Mine: HIT or F. Tap the left half of the world to mine/attack and the right half to place a selected block.

Goal: Explore five biomes, mine deeper ores, craft gear, defeat enemies and bosses, build structures, find Heart Crystals, and survive changing weather.

Heart Crystals: mine them to permanently raise your maximum hearts. Death returns you to your spawn with your world saved.

Progress: enemies become tougher as your score rises. Bosses appear at milestones. The world, tiles and discoveries autosave.
